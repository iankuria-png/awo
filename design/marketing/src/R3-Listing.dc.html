AWO is a free money-learning app for women in Southern Africa and the diaspora.

Know where you stand. A short starter check builds your profile across four areas, explained in plain words.

Learn a little every day. Five-minute lessons, a Vault of money words, and Ola, a friendly guide who explains without judging.

Take one step at a time. Each 30-day plan gives you one clear step each week, then a check-in to see your progress.

Grow together. Share wins, ask questions and find your circle, from stokvels to side hustles.

AWO is for education. It does not give personal financial advice.
