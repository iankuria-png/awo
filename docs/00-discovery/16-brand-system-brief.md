> **Source:** "Visual Brand System Brief" (`AWO_Brand_System_Brief_1.docx`), deliverable 4 of 4 of AWO's social content strategy, prepared for AWO Holdings (Pty) Ltd and shared by Ian on 1 October 2026. It sets design standards for Instagram and LinkedIn.
> **On the canvas:** the page **Brand system (suggested)**: thirteen boards, one per section, drawn in the brief's own system, each with the brief's key elements and worked sample templates. Generator: [`design/brand/`](../../design/brand/README.md).
> **Open:** Q-49, how this system and the app's design language meet.

## The brief in one paragraph

AWO is an **intelligence brand, not a money-tips account**: everything should look at home next to a sophisticated finance publication. The palette, "the Cultural Archive", puts **deep plum** (#4A2D4E) and **terracotta** (#B85C38) on a **warm stone** ground (#F5EBE3), with dusty sage, blush (community only), warm grey (hairlines only) and a plum-tinted body text. Type pairs a **high-contrast serif** for display (Playfair Display in the live assets) with a **neutral grotesque** (Montserrat). Restraint is the brand: one terracotta accent per view, two grounds per piece, no new hues, gradients, shadows or clip-art.

## What the boards add to the brief

- **Contrast checked (WCAG 2.2 AA, a project rule).** Most pairs pass easily (plum on stone 10.06:1). Two pairs the brief relies on are **large-text only**: terracotta on warm stone (3.86:1) and dusty sage on plum (4.44:1), so terracotta key words must be large and sage labels 24 px, or 19 px bold. Terracotta on plum (2.60:1) is a rule, never text, as the brief says. Warm grey (1.55:1) is hairlines only, so a data card's source line uses a darker grey (#6E6470, 4.7:1).
- **Worked samples** on sending money home, a stokvel and a payslip, in line with the app's educational scope. Every figure is labelled as a sample; no statistic is invented.
- **A stand-in wordmark** ("AWO" in the serif with the terracotta rule): the brief sets clear space by the A but doesn't include the artwork (Q-25).

## Where it differs from the app's design language

| | The brief (social) | The app (Round 7 board) |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | Plum, terracotta, warm stone; blush for community | Evergreen, lime, mist, pool, night; blush for Community (D-028, D-039) |
| Ground | Warm stone, a cream | Mist and white; rule D13 treats cream grounds as an AI tell |
| Type | A high-contrast serif and Montserrat | Geist for everything, Kalam for a few handwritten moments (D-032, D-033) |
| Mark | Uppercase AWO (implied by "the height of the A") | Lowercase awo with the stepping-stones mark (D-031; Q-25 open) |
| Tone | Editorial, research house, quietly premium | Warm, plain words, motion-first, fun, modern, clean (D-020, D-028) |

They agree on more than they differ: restraint, one accent, blush kept for community, real dignified women, sourced data, British and South African spelling, and no hype.
