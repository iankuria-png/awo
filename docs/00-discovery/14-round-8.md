## What Round 8 is for

Rounds 5 to 7 settled how AWO looks. Round 8 changes what AWO **does**. Until now the app explained money: where she stands (DIVA), what it means, and one step. Ian wants it to be useful on an ordinary Tuesday, too: when a payslip arrives, when a client hasn't paid, when a colleague mentions ETFs, when a friend is saving for a car. The risk is a drawer of calculators. The aim is the opposite: a small set of tools, each of which leads back into the loop (learn a word, set a goal, share a milestone, check in).

### Local facts found (for design samples only)

These make the samples true to life. Each must be verified and dated before a tool uses it (Q-41).

- **South Africa, 2026/27 tax year:** the first bracket is 18% up to R 245 100; the primary rebate is R 17 820; people under 65 pay no tax below R 99 000 a year. UIF is 1% of pay, capped at R 177,12 a month.
- **Kenya, 2026:** SHIF is 2.75% of gross pay; the Housing Levy is 1.5% (matched by the employer); NSSF is 6% of pensionable pay up to KSh 108 000 (at most KSh 6 480 a month). All three come off before PAYE is worked out.
- **South Africa, VAT:** from 1 April 2026 a business must register once turnover passes R 2,3 million a year (it was R 1 million); voluntary registration starts at R 120 000.
- **Sending money home:** in the third quarter of 2025, sending money from South Africa cost 15.65% on average, the most expensive sending country in the World Bank's survey.
- **Family support:** 44% of South African households supported more than one generation in 2025 (Old Mutual Savings and Investment Monitor).
- **Forex and CFDs:** the EU regulator found 74 to 89% of retail accounts lose money.
- **Crypto in South Africa:** crypto providers need an FSCA licence (300 approved by December 2025). The FSCA fined an unlicensed signal seller for the first time and has called signal selling "the scam du jour".

## The Hub page

**Two modes, one Hub.** A switch at the top: **My job** or **My business**. A member who has both sees both (she picks at sign-up, from the Round 7 "What brings you here", and can change it). The mode reorders the Hub; it never hides a tool.

**The page, top to bottom:**
1. **Title and search:** "What do you want to do?" Search finds tools, Words and lessons ("VAT" finds the word, the setup checklist and the invoice setting).
2. **The next useful thing:** one card, from her own state. For example, "Two invoices are unpaid, R 4 300" or "September's payslip: decode it". Only one.
3. **Pick up where you left off:** drafts (a half-built quote, an unfinished payslip).
4. **Your goals:** several small pools, side by side; tap to open the Goals studio.
5. **Tools by job to be done:** **Understand**, **Plan**, **Grow**, **Run my business** and **Stay safe**. Each group shows its best three, then "All tools".
6. **Stay safe** always comes last and always shows, in both modes.

**The tool skeleton (every tool, the same six beats):**
1. **Your numbers:** typed, or read from a photo or PDF that she confirms line by line.
2. **One big, plain result:** one number and one sentence (rule D7).
3. **What it means:** governed text, "Reviewed by AWO".
4. **Learn this:** a lesson and the Words it used.
5. **Make it a goal or a step.**
6. **Share a milestone:** without amounts.

Calculations are deterministic and versioned, and every tool shows its version and the date of its figures ("Tax tables 2026/27, checked 1 March 2026", as a sample). AI may only read a document or explain; it is labelled "AI-assisted" and logged. **Ola doesn't appear in the Hub** (D-021); Hub AI is plain and labelled, unless Ian decides otherwise (Q-32).

## The new information architecture

| Tab | Shape | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Pool | Today: several goals (pools), this week's step, a Hub shortcut to "the next useful thing", stories and the community pulse |
| **Learn** | Moon | Three tabs: **Paths** (courses and topics), **Words** (the Vault: the arch lives on here) and **Stories** (human stories and the podcast). Always dark (Q-30) |
| **Hub** (centre) | New: see below | Tools that do things, in two modes: My job and My business |
| **Community** | Ripple | A feed ("For you" and "Circles") of Notes, Letters, Milestones, Questions and Shop cards; circles, events and the buddy |
| **Me** | Stones | DIVA profile, progress, report, business profile, settings, and **Your documents** (what she uploaded, and when it will be deleted) |

**Ask** stays a doorway, not a tab: from Home, lessons and Words. Ola lives in Learn (all three tabs) and Ask only.

**Learn, deeper:**
- **Paths:** money basics; pay and tax; debt and credit; goals; saving together; **investing** (shares, dividends, ETFs, unit trusts, bonds, compound growth, fees); **crypto** (what it is, volatility, scams, regulation); **forex** (why most retail traders lose, and the signal-seller scams); property; business; **companies explained** (how a company makes money, what an IPO is, featured African startups as editorial, never an invitation to invest).
- **Words:** the word of the week on the lime arch, practice, collections, and the **buzzword decoder**: type what she heard ("ETF", "pips", "rug pull") and get the Words entry at three depths. If the word isn't there yet, she can ask for it; AI may match spellings and synonyms but never writes a definition.
- **Stories:** Rise stories (failure, then rising); "My worst money mistake" (member submissions, edited, with consent); painful lessons; and the **AWO Podcast** (chapters, a synced transcript, key-lesson cards, low-data audio and offline).

**Community, deeper:** Notes (short, an optional photo), Letters (long form with a title), Milestones (a structured post where a stone lands; amounts hidden by default), Questions (to a circle), Shop cards (from the Shop tool). Reactions: **Cheer**, **Same here** and **Taught me**. No follower counts and no public like totals. Rules as in Round 7: no advice to buy or switch, no recruiting, first names; AI flags and people decide.

## The Hub's icon (batch 1)

Board `R8-Hub-Icons`. Each direction is drawn on the same 24 grid as the other four shapes (a 2px line, round ends), tried in the real tab bar (tap the tabs), on Learn's night ground, at five sizes and blurred.

| Direction | The idea | Could be mistaken for | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keystone** | The wedge that locks an arch, shown at the arch's crown. The Vault's arch moves into Learn as Words; its keystone becomes the Hub, the centre stone in the centre tab | A rainbow or a crown at a glance; the raised wedge keeps it a keystone | **Recommended.** It tells a story members can see, and belongs to the stone and water family |
| **Dial** | A control she turns to fit her numbers | A speedometer, a timer, or a **credit-score gauge**, which breaks rule D8 | Reads instantly as "a tool", but looks like a score |
| **Market awning** | A market stall opening for the day | A shop; it is almost Round 7's Business icon, and employed members may read the Hub as "not for me" | Warm, but says "shop" only |

**First try, rejected:** the keystone drawn alone on two splayed legs read as a little figure at 20px. Five drawings were compared at 20, 24, 48 and 96px beside the other tab icons; only the arch-crown version read as a keystone and stayed distinct from the Words arch (which stands on legs and a base).

**How it moves:** once, when she chooses the Hub tab or the Hub opens (M1).
- **Keystone:** the arch's two halves draw in (300 ms), then the keystone drops in and locks them (450 ms).
- **Dial:** the needle turns to its setting with a small overshoot (700 ms).
- **Awning:** it rolls down (450 ms), then the posts draw (340 ms).

All three ease out; springs stay kept for wins (M4). With reduce motion on, the icon simply appears, complete (M6). If the keystone is picked, the Hub's first opening can play the story once: the keystone lifts out of Learn's arch and settles in the centre tab.

**The Hub's colour:** lime, the Vault's old pill. In AWO lime marks the next step, and the Hub is where she takes it. Words keeps the lime arch inside Learn, on night. Nothing new joins the palette (rule D3).
