## The other screens

- **Welcome:** Oasis v2's layout. Each of its three slides takes the colour and shape of the area it introduces:
  - stones for where you stand;
  - moons for Learn;
  - ripples and faces for Community.
- **Starter check:** the last three sample questions, then "Working out your profile" (on AWO's servers in South Africa; same answers, same result), then the stage.
- **Learn:** the current course and lesson on night, a segmented list (In progress, New, Finished), and Ask Ola in the header.
- **Ask Ola:** Night Oasis's structure: Ola, one question, four suggestion tiles and an input. An answer:
  - is marked AI-assisted;
  - cites its source;
  - suggests follow-ups;
  - never recommends a product.
- **Vault:** the flip card, filter chips (Saving, Borrowing, Business, Sending home) and the word list.
- **Community:**
  - the week's question, where your own answer can be posted;
  - circles, with Safety net linking to Round 4's circle screen;
  - Wanjiru's post with Cheer;
  - a live session with a reminder.
- **Me:** Night Oasis's profile structure in the Me colour:
  - **one readiness arc** (not rings), labelled "not a credit score";
  - **evidence shown separately**;
  - four tappable dimension tiles with their weights;
  - "What this means", with "Explain it more simply" marked AI-assisted;
  - versions as stepping stones (55, 59, 63, then the next check-in);
  - the report and your data.
