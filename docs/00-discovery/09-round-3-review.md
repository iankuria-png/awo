# 09 · Round 3 review: toward focused, human design

> **Status:** Review of Round 3 (canvas page `r3`, 35 boards), 25 Sep 2026.
> **Method:** every board was rendered locally with the canvas's own runtime and reviewed as a screenshot, not from source. Interactive boards were captured in their first state only.
> **Ian's brief for this pass:** "focused, intentional human design: what direction can we explore, what can we cut, what can improve."

## Verdict

Round 3 is a real step up. Direction E reads as a coherent, ownable brand, Ola finally works, and parts of the marketing look close to shippable. But the round is **broad, not deep**:

- There are 35 boards, and nearly every screen tries to do everything.
- The same sample story repeats everywhere.
- "Human" mostly means stock photos inside arches.

The next round should make **fewer screens, with deeper moments**. That means designing the handful of moments where a woman actually *feels* something about her money, including the hard ones.

## Keep: what's working

| What | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Taste board** | Real references, each with one lesson. This is how rounds should start. |
| **Direction E core**: evergreen, lime, blush, Bricolage + Geist | Distinctive, confident, global. Nobody will mistake it for a template bank. |
| **Ola v2** (orb, eyes only, squash and stretch) | Distinctive and warm, and it holds up at 24px. Follows the Headspace lesson without copying it. |
| **"Day for doing, night for learning"** | A clear idea: Learn and Ask are dark and focused; the rest is light. |
| **The pool** (a goal as a vessel that fills), especially on the check-in | The most human data visualisation in the set. Money as water, the oasis made literal. |
| **Member voice**: community posts, the buddy chat | "My gran said: pay yourself before you pay the shop." The words are the most human thing in the round. |
| **Buddy screen** | Two faces, a shared goal and a weekly tick grid: being seen by someone. |
| **Trust moments**: "Stored in South Africa", evidence confidence, "Reviewed by AWO", "Sample" | Provenance is designed in rather than bolted on. |
| **The pensive lock-screen photo** | The only non-smiling image, and the strongest one. |
| **Stokvel "word of the week" ad** and the **pool ad** | Simple, educational and ownable. |

## Cut

1. **Two of the three Homes.** Bento is a dashboard of seven modules, and Story leads with other people. Keep **Timeline** (past → now → next is the loop made visible), trimmed to three items. Bento survives only as widgets.
2. **The ripple-ring DIVA chart.** Four concentric arcs read as Apple's "close your rings". That gamifies the result, which D8 forbids, and the rings are hard to tell apart and label. Lead with the sentence, then show four plain rows.
3. **Metaphor overload.** Ripples, pools, stepping stones, the orb, and Round 2's hand-drawn icons all appear together. Keep **water**: the pool for goals and ripples for feedback. Stones live only in the logo and path, if at all.
4. **The third accent, iris purple, on day-to-day UI** (e.g. the current lesson node). Keep it for Ola's glow only.
5. **Vanity stats** ("9 lessons, 4 actions, 3 check-ins", "+6 points" badges). These are streak mechanics in disguise. Replace them with one human sentence.
6. **Floating UI chips over photos** ("Lesson done", "Safety net R 3 100" on a smiling face). It's the most common fintech-ad trope.
7. **Arches everywhere.** They were Round 2 D's signature, but they've become a crutch. Use them for one moment, not every photo.
8. **More marketing, for now.** The store screenshots, ads and hero are derivative of screens that will change, and they use baked PNGs that already go stale (Store-5 shows R 2 500; the live check-in shows R 1 800). Park them until the product converges.
9. **Repeated sample content.** "Build a starter safety net", "R 3 100" and "What's one money rule…" appear on almost every board, which hides whether the system works for anyone else.

## Improve

1. **One hero per screen.** Every screen should answer "what is the one thing she should notice?" Today most screens have three or four heroes.
2. **Calmer in-app type.** Heavy condensed Bricolage at 40–60px on every page title shouts ("Friday", "Controls", "Hey Naledi"). Keep that size for marketing and big moments; in-app titles should be Bricolage at a medium weight, or Geist.
3. **Give colour one meaning each.** Lime is currently the CTA on dark, wins, badges, stats, word of the week and the stones. Proposed: *lime = progress and wins, plus the primary action on dark*; *evergreen = the primary action on light*; *blush = people only*. Two primary button styles compete on light surfaces today (see the Controls board).
4. **Human voice in the UI copy, not just in the posts.** For example, "You showed up three times this week" instead of "3 learning days".
5. **Emotional range in photography.** Every photo is laughing. Money is also focused, tired, determined and calm. Widen ages (50+), contexts (a salon owner at work, a nurse in a London winter coat, a mother at a kitchen table) and moods. Brief a real shoot (Q-27).
6. **Hard moments are missing.** These are the real test of human design:
   - a score that went **down**,
   - a **missed** week,
   - a "tough month" check-in,
   - **day one**, with nothing yet,
   - coming back after a month away,
   - offline.
7. **Accessibility:**
   - ring labels and several captions are about 10–11px; keep 12px as the floor and 13px for secondary text;
   - check the pale grey timeline dates for contrast;
   - greens that differ only in lightness can't carry meaning alone.
8. **Theme logic.** Decide whether Learn is *always* dark or follows the phone's dark mode. Both is expensive and confusing.
9. **Small defects:**
   - Hero-Device: cream ground (a D13 tell) and a hard-cropped forearm;
   - Learn path: the "Done" label overlaps the line;
   - empty lower halves on Ola, Buddy, Story, Widgets and the Components panels;
   - Round 2's hand-drawn icons quietly became generic line icons in tinted squares. Decide whether to bring the handmade layer back or drop it.

## Directions to explore: three human axes

Each direction is a different answer to "who is AWO to her?"

| Direction | Who AWO is | Feels like | Home is | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mentor** ("quiet companion") | A calm, knowledgeable guide | Headspace, Apple Journal, a good teacher | One card for today, then a gentle timeline | Could feel solitary |
| **Journal** ("her story") | A place to understand yourself | Day One, Stoic, a notebook | Her own timeline: check-ins as chapters, reflections in her words, voice notes | Asks for effort; weaker for low-literacy members without voice |
| **Circle** ("women who get it") | A group of peers | Strava clubs, a WhatsApp group that teaches | Her circle's activity and today's shared question; learning in cohorts | Moderation load; privacy pressure |

**Recommendation:** make **Mentor** the spine and **Circle** the heart. Borrow from Journal the idea that her own words are content. Test the three honestly on one moment before converging.

## Proposed Round 4: five moments, done properly

Instead of 35 boards, about 10, each an interactive flow with its states:

1. **The first result reveal:** the mirror moment, paced one sentence at a time. It asks how she feels, then offers one step. Include a *lower than she hoped* variant.
2. **Doing this week's step:** choose a small action, commit to it, mark it done, and be encouraged by a person (her buddy), not only by confetti.
3. **The 30-day check-in and "what changed":** including a month that went backwards, handled without shame.
4. **A three-minute lesson:** story cards around a named woman's real situation, one question, one reflection, then Ola's celebration.
5. **Being seen:** a buddy cheer, a voice note, a shared milestone in the circle.

Also in Round 4:

- **Three members instead of one**, so the system is tested for range:
  - Naledi, salaried, Johannesburg;
  - Wanjiru, salon owner, Nairobi (entrepreneur context);
  - Amara, nurse, London (diaspora, remittances).
- **Moment 1 in all three directions** (Mentor, Journal, Circle) as the comparison. Then converge.
- **Render and self-review before publishing**, with the local runtime recipe in [06-tooling](06-tooling-and-harness.md).
- Marketing only at the end, built from the live screens.
